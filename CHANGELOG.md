# Changelog

## 0.4.0-trace (prototype, not for main)
- A different way to play the burger week: trace the recipe. The pantry is a grid of ingredient tiles.
- Drag one finger through touching tiles (sideways or diagonally) in recipe order. The order builds on its ticket as you trace.
- Let go on a finished order to serve it, or after two or more tiles to plate part of it and finish it later. Traces of three or more tip extra.
- Used tiles pop and new ones drop in, leaning toward what the orders need. Stuck? Shake the pantry, but every customer waits a little longer.
- Same tickets, tips, street meter, Glossy's stories and feedback as the main game. Burgers only, one week.

## 0.3.0
- Ink Burger grows. The game is now seven weeks long. Each week Glossy's opens something new (pizza, pasta, a deli, a cantina, a bakery, a cookie shop), and Ink Burger adds that dish to its menu.
- The menu mixes: each week serves the new dish plus the two before it. The new dish shows up in about half the orders.
- Each dish is its own suit. Cards only stack on a lower number of the same dish, and every card shows its dish's little icon.
- Cards look like playing cards now: number top left, dish icon top right, and the name on its own line, so long names fit.
- Each dish's new toppings arrive over its first week, like the burger week.
- Win a week and you get a "Week won" card; the next week starts with the street split 60/40 again. The bar at the top names the Glossy's you're up against.
- "Carry on" on the title screen picks up at the start of the day you reached. Anyone who already won the burger week can carry on into week 2.

## 0.2.0
- "Send feedback" on the title screen, the pause screen, and the end-of-day and lose cards. Pick a kind (bug, idea, too hard, too easy, other), write a note, and it goes straight to Otis with the version and where you are in the week attached.
- Back takes you to exactly where you were; a paused shift stays paused while you write.
- If something breaks, a short crash note is sent quietly so it can be fixed.

## 0.1.0
- First playable version, from the concept. Five days, Monday to Friday, against Glossy's across the street.
- Tap a card to add it to the order that needs it next, drag cards between columns, tap the pantry to flip a new card.
- Customers get impatient and walk out; served burgers earn tips and stars, and the meter at the top shows your share of the street.
- Front page with a Play button, and it installs to the home screen like Ink Nine.
- Best run and the sound setting are saved on your phone.
